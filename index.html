Neighborhood marketplace

Welcome to ShareShed

A neighborhood marketplace where people lend and borrow tools instead of buying things that sit unused in a garage.

Project Description

ShareShed connects neighbors so they can list spare tools — drills, ladders, lawnmowers and more — browse what's available nearby, and book pickup or drop-off times, all from one dashboard.

Vision

A neighborhood where every tool is shared, not stored, and every household saves money while reducing waste.

Mission

To make peer-to-peer tool lending simple, trustworthy, and accessible to every local community.
